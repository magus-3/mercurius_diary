## What is Mercurius Diary?

of mixing and flow, being the amalgamation of all things and within all things.

In fact the planet Mercury itself is too an amalgamation of various colours, blue, red and grey...

The Roman God Mercury (derived from the Greek God Hermes) was the God of travel, communication and eloquence, but also trickery...
